Normal Distribution Explorer

Exploration Guide & Learning Objectives

▼
  • Observe the fundamental property: Because the total area must equal 1, a wider spread (larger standard deviation) means the true peak height must decrease. Notice how the curve flattens out and the values on the x-axis spread much further apart to maintain this constant area.
  • Locate the inflection points: Examine where the curve transitions from concave down (curving downward) to concave up (curving upward). These inflection points occur precisely at μ ± σ. Verify this by adjusting the parameters and observing the orange dashed lines. Why might this mathematical property be significant?
  • Verify the Empirical Rule empirically: Use the Probability Calculator to test whether approximately 68% of values fall within μ ± σ. For instance, with μ = 100 and σ = 15, calculate P(X ≤ 115) minus P(X ≤ 85). Does your result align with the theoretical 68%?
  • Explore distribution symmetry: The normal distribution is perfectly symmetric about its mean. Test this claim: for any value k, compare P(X ≤ μ - k) with P(X ≥ μ + k). Use the Probability Calculator to verify that these probabilities are equal for different values of k.
