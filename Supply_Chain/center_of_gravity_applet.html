Center of gravity

Topic 6A · Network footprint design

The idea. Picture the map as a board with a weight on each market. The center of gravity is where the board balances: the volume-weighted average position. Twice the volume counts twice as much, wherever the market sits.

The limit. It gives a first pin, not a site. It does not necessarily minimise distance or cost, and it ignores roads, borders, capacity, service times, risk, labour and real buildings. Test those afterwards on real candidates.

Optional extension: test straight-line burden

Burden = Σ wᵢ dᵢ, with dᵢ the straight-line distance from market i to the site on this sketch. Lower is lighter. It is not a freight bill.
