The Problem: A Skewed Survey

A student survey has 225 responses by programme and year group, but the sample mix is biased. Business is over-represented and IT & Tech is under-represented.

The orange margins are the known enrolment totals. IPF rescales the cells until both row and column totals match those targets while preserving the response pattern inside the table.
